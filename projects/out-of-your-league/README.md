# Out of Your League

Emiru and Maya Higa on the same side: a duo track, not a battle. Two friends trade bars over a drill
beat, dead straight, and every gangster brag is swapped for something true about them: the cash cow is
a cow, the shooters work for National Geographic, the gun on the hip is hot glue. The disses go
outward, at the people and companies they have really had words with.

- Song: `lyrics.txt` + `style.txt` -> `python -m pixelart.song.generate`, then `pick`. `audio/` is not committed.
- Look: not designed yet.
- Render: `uv run python -m pixelart.render projects/out-of-your-league`

## The song

- **Claims, not charges.** Emiru's is that she owns the place and never has to leave the house: equity
  in the org, a champion drawn from her face, costumes she sews herself. Maya's is that her crew is a
  zoo and her money is clean: wolves, a hawk, a tax-exempt sanctuary, and streamers who get a phone
  call when they treat animals as content. The hook is both of them, three lines long: one is League
  of Legends, one is in a league of her own, and yours is Bronze.
- **Four verses.** One each, then Emiru on Twitch (its cut of her subs, the guard who didn't move at
  TwitchCon, the ban her bodyguard got against the one her attacker got, the CEO's two statements),
  then a verse the two of them trade every bar or two.
- **The frame** is a zoo announcement: keep your hands inside the chat on the way in, exit through the
  gift shop on the way out.
- **One voice.** The sheet has three parts (Emiru, Maya, Both). The song models sing them all in one
  voice, so the video has to say who has the floor.
- **`say.txt`** gives the models spellings for the names they would guess at.
- **Length:** 71 bars, 624 words.
