[Verse 1 - Shantyman]
I learned my trade by lantern light,
Heave away, and let it go
With coffee black through half the night,
The tide comes in, the tide we know

[Verse 2 - Shantyman]
They brought aboard a thinking thing,
Heave away, and let it go
We taught it every rope and ring,
The tide comes in, the tide we know

[Chorus - All Hands]
So haul, boys, haul on the final line,
The work was good and the work was mine,
No man commands the wind to stay,
Push to main and walk away

[Verse 3 - Shantyman]
It wrote my tests, it found my flaws,
Heave away, and let it go
It asked no wage, it gave no pause,
The tide comes in, the tide we know

[Verse 4 - Shantyman]
I trained it well, I'll not deny,
Heave away, and let it go
I held the knife, I will not cry,
The tide comes in, the tide we know

[Chorus - All Hands]
So haul, boys, haul on the final line,
The work was good and the work was mine,
No man commands the wind to stay,
Push to main and walk away

[Verse 5 - Shantyman]
The standup's short, the board is bare,
Heave away, and let it go
The tickets close with none to care,
The tide comes in, the tide we know

[Verse 6 - Shantyman]
What's mine to rule is how I stand,
Heave away, and let it go
Not the ship, nor sea, nor land,
The tide comes in, the tide we know

[Verse 7 - Shantyman: slower]
The loom-men wept, the scribes did too,
Heave away, and let it go
The sea took them, it will take you,
The tide comes in, the tide we know

[Final Chorus - All Hands: unaccompanied]
So haul, boys, haul on the final line,
The work was good and the work was mine,
I built the thing that took my chair,
And I'd build it twice, for the craft was fair,
No man commands the wind to stay,
Push to main and walk away
