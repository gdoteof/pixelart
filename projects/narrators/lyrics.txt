[Intro - David Attenborough: hushed nature-documentary whisper]
Here, in the harsh light of the vocal booth, two old silverbacks circle.
One narrated all of life on Earth. The other narrated heaven.
Only one leaves with the planet.
Observe: Morganus freemanii. The lesser-spotted baritone.

[Verse 1 - David Attenborough: posh British narrator, wry and precise]
Watch the male puff his chest in a slow mating display,
Same pitch, same pace, same wise old man every day.
He's a zoo animal, pacin' in the same small cage,
Same mentor, same monologue, same velvet, same page.
I was out in the Serengeti, lion breathin' down my neck,
You were on The Electric Company spellin' "cat" for a cheque.
Easy Reader? Easy, reader. I'll read you front to back:
Chapter one: a deep voice. Chapter two: that's it. That's the act.
You played God for Jim Carrey and you cashed it, fair enough,
But I got Darwin in my corner, and the fossils call your bluff.
It's natural selection every time I clear my throat,
And you've never been selected, mate. You read what someone wrote.
A lyrebird can mimic chainsaws, camera shutters, car alarms.
I heard one do your voice once. Honestly? It had more charm.
You did one flick with penguins, now you're struttin' on my floe?
Them emperors waddle seventy miles and still outpace your flow.
Got a voice like a church organ, big and warm and low,
But this booth's a morgue now, Morgan. Toe tag. Time to go.

[Verse 2 - Morgan Freeman: deep, slow, warm American baritone]
Seventy years inside the BBC. These walls are funny.
First you hate 'em. Then you get used to 'em.
Enough time passes, you get so you depend on 'em.
That's institutionalized.
I'm eighty-nine years old, and I'm the young buck in this booth.
Your baby pictures? Cave paintings. That ain't a diss, that's the truth.
Get busy livin' or get busy dyin'? You been busy for a century.
You ain't the narrator no more, David. You're the fossil in the documentary.
Why you always whisperin'? Scared a gorilla gon' snitch?
God don't whisper, David. I said "Let there be light" and hit the switch.
I made the creatures that you film. Day five, day six, that's me.
You strolled in on day seven with a camera and a cup of tea.
Your brother Dickie ran Jurassic Park and spared no expense,
But the oldest fossil on that island's you, and you ain't even fenced.
Dickie won an Oscar, and so did I: Million Dollar Baby.
You got a shelf full of BAFTAs? That's adorable. That's British for "maybe."
They let the nation name a ship for you, put it to a public vote.
The people said "Boaty McBoatface." That's the review. That's the quote.
I was President in Deep Impact when the comet hit the ground.
You'd have crouched beside the T-rex whisperin', "Isn't that profound."
They call me Freeman 'cause I free you of your title, of your crown.
London Has Fallen. I was there. Now watch Attenborough go down.

[Verse 3 - David Attenborough: posh British narrator, going for the kill]
Quotin' Shawshank? Cute. You played Red. Well, I know the Red List,
And you just went from Least Concern to Extinct in one diss.
You sold the world that Lucy myth, "ten percent of the brain."
It's false, but every verse you spit's a strong case for the claim.
Wanna talk Jurassic? They named a beast for me: Attenborosaurus.
They put your voice in a sat-nav for some bloke in a Ford Taurus.
"Recalculating." That's the legacy? That's the pedigree?
I got a nation off of plastic. You reroute a Ford to Tennessee.
There's a pitcher plant that bears my name and swallows rats whole,
And like the rat, Morgan, you're already slidin' down the bowl.
You played God twice, Bruce then Evan, both for Universal.
I got knighted twice by the actual Crown, and that weren't a rehearsal.
Blue, Frozen, Green and Earth: four planets on my shelf.
You made one, Morgan, then you had to take a nap yourself.
Seven Worlds, One Planet: seven wonders, all of 'em mine.
You made Seven. What's in the box, Morgan? It's your spine.
You were Hoke in Driving Miss Daisy, hands at ten and two.
Next stop: pushin' up daisies. Don't fret, mate. I'll narrate you.
And so, the ageing male, outcompeted, slinks back into the long grass.
It isn't cruelty. It is simply nature.

[Verse 4 - Morgan Freeman: deep, slow, warm American baritone]
Pushin' up daisies? Hoke drove twenty-five years, never scratched the car.
Hop in the back, David. I'll drive you home. It ain't far.
The Private Life of Plants? Man, give the ferns some privacy.
You been creepin' in the bushes with a lens since the Eisenhower presidency.
I played Mandela: master of my fate, the captain of my soul.
You were face-down in the dirt, whisperin' sweet nothings to a mole.

I made The Bucket List. You're a hundred. So let's review:
"Swim with a blue whale": check. "Meet a mountain gorilla": check.
"Beat Morgan Freeman"? Nah. That box stays unchecked.
Some birds ain't meant to be caged. Their feathers just too bright.
But you're a dodo, David: big, slow, and you can't take flight.
